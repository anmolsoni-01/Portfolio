About

I am a Computer Science and Engineering student at Lovely Professional University, currently specializing in Artificial Intelligence and Data Analytics. My academic foundation spans core programming languages, data analysis, and applied AI concepts, which I continue to strengthen through structured coursework and independent projects.

My technical development is grounded in practical application rather than theory alone. I have built and delivered a command-line game in Python, a real-time digital clock in JavaScript, and contributed to a team-developed IoT-based food freshness detection system built on Arduino and multiple sensor modules. This experience, alongside competitive boxing at the district level, has shaped my approach to leadership, discipline, and adaptability, and reinforced the value of teamwork and clear communication — qualities I carry into both academic and technical environments.
